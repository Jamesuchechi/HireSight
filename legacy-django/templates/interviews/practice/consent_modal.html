Video Recording Consent

Please review our privacy practices before starting your practice session

Welcome to HireSight Practice Sessions! Before we proceed, we want to be transparent about how we use video during your practice interviews. Your privacy and data security are our top priorities.

What Video Data We Collect

Video Recording: Your face, body language, and gestures during your answer
Audio Recording: Your voice and verbal responses to interview questions
Metadata: Session date, duration, questions asked, and your responses
Behavioral Metrics: Eye contact, head pose, blink rate, speaking pace (AI analysis only)

How We Use Your Data

AI Analysis: Analyze your performance to provide personalized feedback
Score Generation: Evaluate your answers against interview criteria
Progress Tracking: Monitor your improvement over multiple practice sessions
NOT Sold or Shared: We never sell your data to third parties

Data Retention

Video Files: Stored for 30 days, then automatically deleted

Performance Data: Retained for 2 years to track long-term progress

User Control: You can request deletion of your data at any time

Security & Privacy Measures

  • End-to-end encryption for video transmission
  • Encrypted storage on secure servers
  • No raw video URLs exposed to anyone
  • Signed URLs with 15-minute expiration for access
  • GDPR compliant data handling

Your Rights

✓ Access: Request a copy of all your data

✓ Correction: Update inaccurate information

✓ Deletion: Request permanent deletion of your data

✓ Opt-Out: Disable video analysis (scores will not be available)

✓ Withdraw: Withdraw consent at any time
