Communication Timeline

Timeline
  1. Immediate: Submit this response to trigger the notification to the recruiter.
  2. Within 5 minutes: Recruiter reviews your proposed slot and accepts/reschedules.
  3. 15 minutes later: Candidate receives confirmation email with updated calendar invite.
  4. Ongoing: Panel hosts are reminded 24h/1h before using automated reminders.
  5. Follow-up: Check the interview detail page for further updates and completion status.

Communication Tips

  • Focus on facts: share availability window and reason.
  • Keep notes short to avoid email overload.
  • Reference the job title & original date for clarity.
  • Feel free to share alternative slots after the initial proposal.

Reminders

  • Zoom link is reused unless you request a new link.
  • On-site addresses remain active until cancelled.
  • Reschedules maintain the same interviewers unless updated manually.
If you need additional slots beyond this form, coordinate directly through your recruiter or hiring team channel.

Candidate Preparation Notes

Before the interview, make sure the candidate receives:

  • A quick agenda outlining who will speak and expected duration.
  • Any code or homework links needed ahead of time.
  • Contact information for changes (email + phone).

We automatically attach whatever is in the Candidate Instructions field of the interview record. Update that field before submitting if you need to share new details.

Accessibility & Amenities

Let us know if you require disabilities accommodations or specific room setup so we can update the shared calendar invite.
